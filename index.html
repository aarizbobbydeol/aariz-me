Who I am

I'm a builder before I'm anything else. I like the whole arc of a thing — from a blank repo to something real people use.

Most people pick one lane: the ML, or the frontend, or the marketing. I kept refusing to. Over the last two years I've shipped computer-vision and LLM systems, built and deployed nearly two dozen websites, and run the SEO and automation that turned them into leads and revenue — often on the same project. That range is the point of me.

I'm most alive at the intersection: where research becomes a product, where a model becomes a business, where an idea becomes a live URL that ranks #1 on Google. I care about things that actually ship — not demos, not slides.
