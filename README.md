# Mohd Aariz Amaan - Personal Portfolio

My personal portfolio - who I am, what I have built, what I am building, and where I am headed.

**Live:** https://aariz-me.vercel.app

## About
B.Tech (Data Science & AI) graduate working at the meeting point of AI technology and finance. I build things end to end - AI systems, production websites, and the growth behind them.

## Tech
HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no build step. Fraunces + Inter + Newsreader type, dark editorial theme, deployed on Vercel.

## Pages
- `/` home, with the ship log: a canvas line with a marker for each thing I shipped
- `/projects` all 15 public repos, filterable, with screenshot previews of the live sites (`img/shots/`)
- `/experience` jobs, education and certifications
- `/about` story, interests, goals and contact
- `/work/changewatch`, `/work/rag-assistant`, `/work/autoeval-ai` case studies

`vercel.json` turns on clean URLs, so `/about` serves `about.html`.
